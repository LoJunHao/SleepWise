🎨 Visual & Experience Design
Science-Centric Palette: Built on a calming, high-contrast dark visual system using Deep Calming Navy (#16213E), Vibrant Electric Blue (#2B7FFF), and Muted Lavender (#B7B5FF) accents, creating a soothing aesthetic suitable for late-night viewing.
Modern Typography: Paired elegant, modern Space Grotesk for prominent headers/numerical readings with highly legible Inter for descriptions and instructions, alongside JetBrains Mono for technical telemetry metrics (such as hours and milligrams).
Smooth Navigation: Added scrolling controls. Clicking the top-level feature cards or the primary Hero button "Calculate Now" triggers a smooth scroll directly to the main scientific workspace dashboard.
🧬 Modular Scientific Features Implemented
Bedtime & Wake-Up Calculator (Features 1 & 2):
Formulates bedtime and wake targets utilizing customizable sleep onset latency (5–30 mins) and average sleep cycle lengths (70–120 mins) to emphasize research-supported variation rather than rigid 90-minute cycle myths.
Sleep Duration & Efficiency Analyzer (Feature 3):
Translates bedtime and wake inputs into absolute minutes asleep.
Dynamically deducts typical micro-awakenings (15 mins) to produce a realistic clinical Sleep Efficiency Ratio (%) accompanied by National Sleep Foundation guideline summaries.
Weekly Sleep Debt Aggregator (Feature 4):
Provides a slider-driven weekly calendar log (Monday–Sunday) to enter hours slept.
Compiles cumulative debt and displays conservative, circadian-safe sleep extension advice, warning against excessive single-night sleeping-in.
Circadian Chronotype Quiz (Feature 5):
Features a 5-question interactive evaluation to categorize users into Morning Larks, Intermediate Bears, or Night Owls.
Outputs custom, evidence-backed daily routine structures including peak cognitive focus hours, physical workout windows, caffeine cutoffs, and daylight anchor suggestions.
Weighted Sleep Hygiene Score (Feature 6):
An interactive environmental and behavioral audit (Cool bedroom, blackout dark, no caffeine, screen reduction) calculating a weighted Score out of 100 with science-grounded optimization tips.
Sleep Consistency & Social Jet Lag Tracker (Feature 7):
Evaluates sleep schedules across Workdays vs. Weekend Days.
Calculates a clinical Sleep Regularity Index (SRI).
Renders a highly interactive, custom SVG Timeline Gantt Chart visually highlighting circadian misalignment (social jet lag) boundaries.
Restorative Nap Planner (Feature 8):
Recommends target naps based on daily objectives: 20-min Power Nap (alertness), 35-min Cognitive Reboot, or 90-min Full Cycle (learning/REM consolidation).
Explains deep-sleep stage transitions and includes a guide on the research-backed "Coffee Nap" strategy.
Caffeine Clearance Decay Simulator (Feature 9):
Tracks beverage consumption (Coffee, Espresso, Tea, Energy Drink, Soda, or custom mg) and plots an interactive, beautifully curved SVG Caffeine Decay Chart over 15 hours post-drink, based on metabolic half-lives.
Estimates bedtime caffeine load and grades sleep stage disruption hazards.
Jet Lag Phase Shifter (Feature 10):
Assembles timezone phase shift templates for transmeridian travelers, suggesting daylight exposure/avoidance timings and melatonin dosage, supported by prominent clinical medical disclaimers.
